## Findings — most severe first

### F01 — High: the screens do not form a navigable journey

**Screens:** all five. **Evidence:** `src/app/page.tsx:10`, `src/app/page.tsx:13`; `src/app/sheet/page.tsx:78`; `src/app/scan/ScanClient.tsx:237`, `:291`, `:408`; `src/components/results/ResultsDemoClient.tsx:65`; `src/app/account/page.tsx:33`; baseline screenshots above.

Home has **one link**, to Account, which currently has **zero controls or links** and says “Sign-in unavailable.” Sheet likewise has **zero controls or links**. Scan has one Home link, no way to obtain the sheet it requires, a false “Step 1 of 3,” and a success branch that ends after measurements. Results offers fixture/disclosure controls but no route out. This is more than unfinished visual polish. The “coming later” home copy is honest about unfinished integration, but the shipped pages cannot be discovered or followed as a measurement journey.

**Standard:** wayfinding/checklist (`docs/design-guidelines.md:24`, `:107`) and actionable errors (`:104`). **Fix:** provide a consistent Home/Back exit and links between available steps; give Scan a “Get the sheet” action. Use an accurate one-photo label. Make the unavailable-account state explain that sign-in is temporarily unavailable and offer one usable action, such as returning to the measurement flow. Add the real results continuation when integration exists, with an honest interim completion state. Do not imply results are saved or available before that is true.

### F02 — High: a model-download error leaves Scan permanently processing

**Screen:** `/scan`. **Evidence:** `src/app/scan/ScanClient.tsx:98`, `:108`, `:148`; `src/client/photo/landmarks.ts:29`, `:88`; [failure screenshot](ui-audit/scan-390-dark-model-failure-stuck.png).

With Playwright aborting only `/mediapipe/models/**`, selecting a synthetic sheet image produced **two “Failed to fetch” page errors**, **zero retake error panels**, and the status remained “Looking for the sheet markers and your hand…” after **3 seconds**. The source explains why this cannot recover by itself: neither warm-up nor `runPipeline` catches the rejection, and `landmarkerPromise` retains the rejected promise. This is fault injection, not a claim that normal production downloads were failing.

**Standard:** visible truthful status and problem plus corrective action (`docs/design-guidelines.md:26`, `:32`, `:103`, `:104`). **Fix:** catch initialization/pipeline failures, clear a failed cached model promise, and show a specific error with one Retry action that retries asset loading. Preserve the chosen photo. End the processing state on every failure.

### F03 — High: the anonymous deletion statement is not backed by the documented timing mechanism

**Screen:** `/account`. **Evidence:** `src/app/account/page.tsx:18`; [copy](ui-audit/account-390-light-reduce.png); `docs/PLAN.md:213`; `src/server/scans/cookies.ts:39`; `src/server/scans/submit.ts:7`, `:68`, `:88`; `src/server/scans/drizzle-repo.ts:24`, `:88`; `src/app/BeaconOnUnload.tsx:33`; `vercel.json:9`.

The UI says **“deleted within 24 hours of closing your browser.”** It does not claim immediate deletion, which is good. However, it does not explain browser-session access ending separately from server cleanup. Source sets expiry **24 hours after session creation**, attempts deletion on `pagehide`, and otherwise deletes expired rows on subsequent scan writes or a **daily** cron. Expired reads are rejected, but that is different from deleting stored rows.

Source-derived counterexample, not a production retention experiment: a session created just after the daily sweep, followed by an immediate close whose beacon is lost and no subsequent scan traffic, is not expired at the next sweep just under 24 hours later. Physical removal can then approach **48 hours after creation/close**. The write-triggered sweep reduces this delay under traffic; it does not guarantee a 24-hour bound when quiet. PLAN still describes an hourly sweep, while STATUS records the later daily-backstop decision (`docs/STATUS.md:140`).

**Standard:** precise/private copy (`docs/design-guidelines.md:8`) and the explicit honesty requirement in `docs/PLAN.md:214`. **Fix:** Claude should reconcile the deletion mechanism with the promised bound and verify it, rather than silently weaken the promise. Explain cookie/session access ending and server cleanup as separate events in plain language. Only state a “within 24 hours” deletion guarantee once that maximum is actually enforced. This audit did not access or delete production records.

### F05 — Medium: the phone sheet preview has an unreachable left edge

**Screen:** `/sheet` at 390 px. **Evidence:** `src/app/sheet/sheet.css:22`; [initial](ui-audit/sheet-390-light-reduce.png); [scrolled right](ui-audit/sheet-390-light-scrolled-right.png).

The SVG correctly retains its **793.69 CSS px / 210 mm** width. But the centered overflowing flex child begins at **x = −201.84 px**. The 390 px container has scroll width **608 px**, scroll range **0–218 px**, and assigning a negative scroll position clamps to zero. The leftmost **201.84 px cannot be reached**, including the left top-down markers. This is not a request to scale down the calibrated sheet.

**Standard:** exact-size preview and accessible presentation (`docs/design-guidelines.md:19`, `:91`). **Fix:** preserve the 210 mm SVG and print geometry, but start-align it on narrow screens or center it only when it fits. Ensure the scroll range covers the entire sheet; provide a clear horizontal-scroll affordance.

### F06 — Medium: control targets and upload keyboard focus need completion

**Screens:** `/`, `/scan`. **Evidence:** `src/app/page.tsx:13`; `src/app/scan/ScanClient.tsx:292`, `:372`; `src/app/scan/scan.css:164`, `:237`; [upload focused](ui-audit/scan-390-light-keyboard-focus-reduce.png).

The Home account link measures **184.48 × 21 px**, below the standard's 44 px target height. The upload input is keyboard reachable and has an accessible name, but its native focus ring is clipped into a **1 × 1 px** visually hidden box; its visible label has no focus outline or box shadow. This is a visibility failure, not a claim that keyboard upload is impossible. Manual card handles also have no enlarged hit area: for a 3000 px image rendered at the phone's 342 px stage width, the source radius produces an **8.21 px circle diameter** before its thin stroke, with no 44 px hit region (source-derived, not a rendered manual-card measurement).

**Standard:** reachable controls and targets at least 44 × 44 px (`docs/design-guidelines.md:97`, `:99`). **Fix:** enlarge the Home link hit area, reflect input `:focus-visible` on the visible upload control, and give each card handle an invisible 44 px minimum hit region independent of the photo's coordinate scale.

### F07 — Medium: the deletion confirmation declares a modal without managing keyboard focus

**Screen:** signed-in `/account`; **source-only finding**. **Evidence:** complete dialog branch at `src/app/account/AccountView.tsx:125`, opener at `:84`, Cancel at `:148`.

The dialog uses `aria-modal="true"` and a dim backdrop, but has no initial focus, focus containment, Escape handling, focus restoration, or background inerting. Opening it leaves focus on “Delete everything” behind the backdrop. `aria-modal` alone does not implement those behaviors. The dimming requirement is met (`src/app/globals.css:148`).

**Standard:** accessible modal task and a clear way to leave (`docs/design-guidelines.md:79`, `:91`, `:107`). **Fix:** use a properly managed native dialog or accessible dialog primitive; initially focus Cancel, keep keyboard focus inside, support Escape before submission, and restore focus to the opener. Production sign-in was unavailable, so live dialog operation is not claimed.

### F08 — Medium: photo processing still blocks the main thread

**Screen:** `/scan`. **Evidence:** `src/app/scan/ScanClient.tsx:145`; `src/client/photo/pipeline.ts:119`, `:123`, `:154`, `:198`; `src/client/photo/landmarks.ts:89`; [resulting error state](ui-audit/scan-390-light-markers-error-reduce.png).

A focused production measurement using a **3000 × 2000** synthetic markerless PNG recorded a **425 ms main-thread long task** after selection. PNG creation occurred before the observer was installed. This is one desktop Chromium observation, not a phone performance benchmark. Source runs image extraction, marker/card detection and landmark detection on the page thread. Yielding once before starting allows a status paint but does not keep the interface responsive throughout detection.

**Standard:** “never a frozen UI while detection runs”; off-main-thread work where possible (`docs/design-guidelines.md:26`). **Fix:** move detection/image processing into a worker where supported, retain immediate status, and let replacement/cancellation supersede pending work. Check representative phone photos on actual hardware afterward.

### F09 — Medium: changing the hand picker does not apply the correction an error recommends

**Screen:** `/scan`; **source-only state-transition finding**. **Evidence:** `src/client/photo/gates.ts:94`; `src/app/scan/ScanClient.tsx:108`, `:139`, `:260`, `:281`.

The mismatch error offers “change the hand picker.” That control only calls `setHand`; it does not reprocess the retained file or clear/revalidate the previous result. The user must additionally choose the photo again, an unstated step. Changing grip likewise leaves an already-produced submission based on its old value. Also, replacement has no operation/version guard: an older asynchronous run can publish its result after a newer photo has been selected.

**Standard:** errors with an action that fixes the problem, and safe replacement (`docs/design-guidelines.md:32`, `:34`). **Fix:** revalidate the retained photo when relevant settings change, or expose one explicit Recheck action. Commit results only for the current photo/settings generation and preserve a valid previous capture until its replacement succeeds.

### F10 — Medium: the best match has no way to inspect its six scores

**Screen:** `/results/demo`. **Evidence:** `src/components/results/TopPick.tsx:18`, `:31`; `src/components/results/RankedList.tsx:21`, `:57`; [top pick](ui-audit/results-1280-light-reduce.png).

**Standard:** the six sub-scores should sit one level deeper (`docs/design-guidelines.md:43`). **Fix:** add a “See all fit details” disclosure on the best match containing all six scores/reasons, keeping the concise recommendation first.

### F11 — Medium: disclosure motion violates the property and transition rules even though it is interruptible

**Screens:** results; account dialog also has a source-only motion mismatch. **Evidence:** `src/components/results/results.css:310`, `:325`, `:340`, `:409`; `src/app/globals.css:167`, `:177`.

Results animates `grid-template-rows` for **320 ms** with a fixed cubic-bezier curve, rather than using the permitted transform/opacity properties and spring behavior. Closing immediately sets inner content to `visibility: hidden`, so content disappears before the space finishes collapsing. Reduced motion changes the duration to **0.01 ms**, with no cross-fade. The account dialog similarly uses a fixed **200 ms** scale keyframe for entry.

The disclosure **does pass interruptibility**: a second press around 67 ms into opening was accepted, made the region closed/inert, and it settled to 0 px. This finding does not claim that CSS transitions necessarily block input.

**Standard:** springs for touched interactions, spatially consistent entry/exit, animate only transform/opacity, and reduced-motion cross-fades (`docs/design-guidelines.md:59`, `:67`, `:69`, `:93`). **Fix:** use an interruptible transform/opacity treatment or a simple fade/static disclosure consistent with the agreed standard; retain content through the visible exit and use a reduced-motion cross-fade. For the dialog, remove unnecessary scale motion or implement the prescribed spring treatment.

### F12 — Medium: internal vocabulary leaks into accessible scan content and reusable results copy

**Standard:** no internal vocabulary and accessible results (`docs/design-guidelines.md:45`, `:97`, `:108`). **Fix:** put test payloads in non-announced data attributes or hidden test hooks, leaving the labeled list for people. Use “We haven't assessed this mouse's shape yet” and “This ranking may change as we learn more about its shape.” The demo's explicit `ResultsView` / `FitResponse` / fixture controls are reviewer tooling; they should not ship in the eventual user results route, but their presence here is not itself counted as a product-copy defect.

## Guideline drift and claims

### 1. Three photos versus one

**Shipped behavior is one top-down photo slot**, confirmed by the single production file input and `src/app/scan/ScanClient.tsx:291`. `docs/STATUS.md:138` records the 2026-09-22 deferral of side/grip photos. The three-slot heading and “step n of 3” rule at `docs/design-guidelines.md:22` are now out of date. Missing side/grip slots are **not** implementation failures.

The UI still inherits stale workflow language: “Step 1 of 3” (`src/app/scan/ScanClient.tsx:241`) and “Side and grip photos come later” (`:247`). The printed sheet also still instructs a side-shot fold (`src/app/sheet/page.tsx:119`, `:145`). Revise guidance to match the active top-down task and clarify that the side-shot setup is deferred; do not casually change calibrated marker geometry. Replacement, local processing, overlay feedback and error requirements still apply to the one retained slot.

### 2. The 3D viewer

**M4b does not exist yet and is blocked on Blender assets. It is not graded.** STATUS identifies it as the next asset-dependent work (`docs/STATUS.md:16`, `:23`). The viewer rules at `docs/design-guidelines.md:49` remain prospective requirements, not evidence that a shipped screen has failed orbit/zoom/momentum behavior.

### 3. Privacy and deletion

**Photo locality passes within the inspected implementation and exercised paths.** The promise appears beside photo selection (`src/app/scan/ScanClient.tsx:302`) and in sheet instructions (`src/app/sheet/page.tsx:83`). Preview uses an object URL (`src/app/scan/ScanClient.tsx:144`); decoding/detection use browser APIs (`src/client/photo/decode.ts:67`; `src/client/photo/pipeline.ts:103`). The hand model and WASM are same-origin downloads (`src/client/photo/landmarks.ts:18`). After asset loading settled, corrupt-image, markerless-image and synthetic-sheet processing collectively generated **zero HTTP requests** in the observation window.
